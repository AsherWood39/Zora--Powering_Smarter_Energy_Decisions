========================================================================
ZORA: THE STRATEGIC BRAIN (Technical Summary for Presentation)
========================================================================

1. PROJECT AIM & VISION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Zora is a bridge between Battery Science and Operational Excellence. 
It transforms raw battery data (voltage, temp, current) into clear 
"Maintenance Directives."

AIM: Move from "Reactive Maintenance" (fixing things when they break) 
to "Prescriptive Maintenance" (knowing exactly what to do to prevent 
failure).

2. TECHNICAL STACK (The Architecture)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- FRONTEND: Vue 3 (Logic), Vite (Speed), Chart.js (Visuals).
- BACKEND: Flask (API Gateway), Pandas/NumPy (Data Guru).
- AI/ML: 
  * XGBoost Engine: Predicts health (SoH) and life (RUL).
  * Llama 3 Expert: Turns numbers into human instructions.

3. TECHNICAL FORMULAS & PHYSICS (The Science)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* FORMULA 1: State of Health (SoH)
  SoH (%) = (Current Capacity / Nominal Capacity) * 100
  - Warning Threshold: < 80% (The "Degradation Knee")
  - Critical Threshold: < 70% (End-of-Life for EVs)

* FORMULA 2: Internal Resistance (Re)
  Resistance is the primary indicator of physical wear.
  Power Loss = Current^2 * Resistance
  - Zora Rule: If Resistance > 0.085 Ohms, trigger "Critical Decay" 
    directive.

* FORMULA 3: The Simulation Engine (Digital Twin)
  Adjusted Life = Base Life * (1.0 - Total Penalties)
  - Thermal Penalty: 24C is optimal.
  - Cold Penalty: 1.8% loss per degree away from 24C.
  - Heat Penalty: 1.0% loss per degree away from 24C.

4. KEY INNOVATIONS (The "X-Factor")
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* ACTION-ORIENTED AI: 
  - Title: Direct Command (e.g., "Limit Charge Current to 0.4C")
  - Description: Scientific Why (e.g., "Resistance has spiked 15%")

* FLEET TRIAGE:
  Automatically sorts the whole fleet into:
  - GREEN: Healthy (SoH > 80%)
  - YELLOW: Warning (70-80%)
  - RED: Critical (< 70%)

* ENGINEERING EXPORTS:
  One-click PDF reports including visual trend graphs so technicians 
  have proof of impending failure points.

5. SUMMARY STATEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Zora uses physics-informed AI to quantify exactly what is happening 
inside battery cells, then translates those formulas into actionable 
maintenance strategies for fleet operators."
